My mother has stage IV terminal lung and brain cancer. It is a great wish of hers to find more information on the man who was her father.

What we do know:

  • Not the famous general Vicente Lim
  • Was a Chinese businessman whose family has ties to Fujian, China
  • Was in the logging business in Barangay Careal, near Botolan, Zambales, Philippines
  • Was born before 1930
  • My mother was born in the mid to late 1950s in Botolan, Zambales
  • He moved to or near Santa Cruz, Zambales after she was born
